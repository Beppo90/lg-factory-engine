## 5. WHILE DETALLADO

### WHILE-A — PM-2.10 Act 1: Discovery First + Error Log (0:15–1:00 · 45 min)

**⚠️ OBLIGATORIO: Discovery First antes de proyectar el Formula Box completo.**

**0:15–0:25 · Noticing en texto de S2 (10 min):**
**TT:**
> *"Take the text from last class — 'Tools Every Diesel Specialist Needs'. Three tasks: (1) Underline all imperatives — green. (2) Circle all 'There is/There are' phrases — blue. (3) Box all prepositions of place — red. You have 8 minutes."*

**ICQ:** *"How many tasks? — Three. What color for imperatives? — Green. For There is/are? — Blue. For prepositions? — Red. Start."*

**0:25–0:35 · Construcción del cuadro resumen (10 min):**
Instructor construye en pizarra CON el grupo:
> *"What imperatives did you find? ... Good. So the rule is: [Verb] + [Object]. No subject. Now negative: Don't + [Verb]. Who found one? ..."*

💡 **Proyectar Formula Box SOLO como confirmación** — después que el grupo haya inferido la regla.

**0:35–1:00 · Error Log (25 min):**
**TT:**
> *"Here is the Error Log — 10 sentences from the workshop. Each has ONE grammar error. Find it and correct it. Use the Formula Box. Work alone — 15 minutes."*

**ICQ:** *"How many errors per sentence? — One. Can you use the Formula Box? — Yes. Work alone or with partner? — Alone. Time? — 15 minutes. Start."*

Error Log (para referencia del instructor):

| # | Oración con error | Corrección | Target |
|---|------------------|------------|--------|
| 1 | *Check the floor jack for leak.* | *...for leaks.* | Imperativo — objeto (plural) |
| 2 | *Don't to leave tools on the floor.* | *Don't leave...* | Don't + base form |
| 3 | *There is tools on the workbench.* | *There are tools...* | There are + plural |
| 4 | *There are a spill near bay 2.* | *There is a spill...* | There is + singular |
| 5 | *The torque wrench is in the workbench.* | *...on the workbench.* | on (superficie) |
| 6 | *The floor jack is next the bay door.* | *...next to the bay door.* | next to (no omitir 'to') |
| 7 | *Don't checks the toolbox without gloves.* | *Don't check...* | base form (no -s) |
| 8 | *There is no goggles in the bay.* | *There are no goggles...* | There are no + plural |
| 9 | *Replace the filter in the toolbox.* | **CORRECTA** ✓ | Comprensión check |

### WHILE-B — PM-2.10 Act 2: Grammar Stations (1:00–1:50 · 50 min)

**Logística: 3 estaciones × 12 min + 3 min transición × 2 = 30 min rotación. Buffer 20 min para setup y conclusiones.**

**1:00–1:05 · Setup de estaciones:**
**TT:**
> *"Three stations. Group 1 starts at Station A. Group 2 at Station B. Group 3 at Station C. 12 minutes at each station. I'll signal the rotation. Go to your first station — now."*

**ICQ:** *"How long at each station? — 12 minutes. Do you move at the same time? — Yes, when I signal. What do you do if you finish early? — Check your partner's work. Ready?"*

**Station A — Imperativo (12 min):**
Tarjetas con instrucciones de seguridad incompletas. Completar con el verbo correcto del Language Bank:
- *"___ the gloves before starting."* → *Check*
- *"___ the bolts after the repair."* → *Tighten*
- *"___ the filter every 10,000 km."* → *Replace*
- *"___ the workbench at the end of the day."* → *Clean*
- *"Don't ___ the lift without checking first."* → *use*
- *"Don't ___ tools on the floor."* → *leave*

**Station B — There is/are (12 min):**
Imagen de bay con hazards. Producir 4 oraciones describiendo condiciones:
- *"There is a ___ near ___."*
- *"There are ___ on the ___."*
- *"There is no ___ in ___."*
- *"Is there a ___ in ___?"*

**Station C — Preposiciones de lugar (12 min):**
Layout del taller con 8 herramientas indicadas. Escribir oración con preposición:
- *"The torque wrench is ___ the workbench."* → on
- *"The floor jack is ___ Bay 1."* → next to
- *"The toolbox is ___ the workbench."* → under
- *"The PPE is ___ the bay door."* → near
- *"The socket is ___ the toolbox."* → in

**1:50 · Cierre de estaciones:**
> *"All done. The Formula Box is your tool for Bay 2. Now — practice run."*

### WHILE-D — PM-2.4 Act 1: Genre Analysis + Modeled Writing (2:45–3:15 · 30 min)

**2:45–2:55 · Genre Analysis (10 min):**
**TT:**
> *"Now we look at the real checklist model. (Proyectar y distribuir modelo A4.) 3 minutes — answer these questions: How many columns? What verbs do you see? What is the status column? What is the observation column?"*

Construir cuadro resumen en pizarra con el grupo:
- Estructura: Ítem | Instrucción (imperativo) | Estado (✓ / ✗ / N/A) | Observación (There is/are)
- Verbos frecuentes: Check, Inspect, Replace, Clean, Tighten

**2:55–3:15 · Modeled Writing — Think-Aloud (20 min):**
**TT:**
> *"Now I write the first 3 items of the Bay 2 checklist — on the board. I think aloud. Watch and listen."*

Instructor completa 3 ítems en voz alta:
> *"Item 1: What do I check first in Bay 2? The floor jack. So: 'Check the floor jack.' Why imperative? Because it's an instruction for the technician. Status: ✓ — it's OK. Observation: 'No leaks detected.' Item 2: Gloves and goggles — PPE. 'Check the PPE — gloves and goggles.' Status: ✓. No observation needed. Item 3: Spill near the door. 'Inspect the bay door area.' Status: ✗. Observation: 'There is an oil spill near the bay door.'"*

💡 **Obligatorio:** Instructor completa el think-aloud con los 3 ítems en la pizarra. Los aprendices copian/observan. Estos 3 ítems son el scaffold de los 8 que deben producir.

## 8. DIFFERENTIATION NOTES

### Fast Finishers
- **Error Log:** crear 2 oraciones propias con error deliberado → dar al compañero para corregir.
- **Producción:** añadir una sección de "Required Actions" al checklist usando imperativos negativos (Don't...).
- **Peer Review:** escribir un feedback completo de 3–4 frases por criterio (no solo ✓/✗).

### Need More Support
- **Discovery First:** si el grupo no puede inferir la regla del imperativo → OK usar la L1 para verificar comprensión de la regla, pero el Formula Box se presenta igual como confirmación.
- **Producción individual:** reducir el mínimo a 6 ítems (no 8) si hay bloqueo de producción. Mantener el mínimo de 2 observaciones.
- **Error Log:** trabajar en pares (no individual) si el nivel de competencia es muy bajo. Los dos comparten la misma hoja.
- **Peer Review:** aprendices pueden usar L1 para comentar oralmente al par PERO el feedback escrito en la lista de verificación debe ser en inglés.

### Chunk Bank — 5 Communicative Functions

**F1 — Giving instructions** *(use an imperative verb)*
> "Check the floor jack before starting."
> "Wear your goggles."
> "Don't leave tools on the floor."

**F2 — Requesting tools or help** *(use Can I / I need)*
> "Can I have the torque wrench, please?"
> "I need the socket set for Bay 2."

**F3 — Describing conditions** *(use There is/are + preposition)*
> "There is a spill near the bay door."
> "The fire extinguisher is next to the workbench."
> "There are tools on the floor."

**F4 — Reporting to supervisor** *(open and close your report)*
> "Good morning, Carlos. Bay 2 is ready."
> "Bay 2 is not ready. There is a problem with the ___."
> "I inspected Bay 2. Everything is in order."

**F5 — Confirming understanding** *(close your report or respond)*
> "Understood."
> "Got it. I'll do it now."
> "Sorry, can you repeat that?"

---

### Suggested Report Structure *(bullets only — NOT a script)*

1. **Open** (F4): *"Good morning, Carlos. Bay ___ is ready / not ready."*
2. **Conditions** (F3): *"There is/are ___ [condition]. The ___ is ___ [preposition]."*
3. **Actions** (F1/Gr3): *"I checked the ___. I inspected the ___."*
4. **Problem, if any** (F3+F4): *"There is a problem with the ___. I reported it."*
5. **Close** (F5): *"Bay ___ is ready to start. Awaiting your approval."*

## A.2 Guide Design DNA

### Nombre conceptual
**The Maritime Operations Pro — Andrea's 100-Hour Maritime Journey**

### Enfoque y conceptualización
Curso intensivo de actualización profesional que comprime los 4 RAPs SOFÍA (vocablos básicos · SMCP · gramática puerto-buque · descripción de funciones) en una sola guía de 100h. El aprendiz llega con prereq A1.1 cubierto y sale con capacidad operacional inmediata como Port Operations Trainee — capaz de comprender VHF de un Vessel Master no-nativo, dirigir maniobras de atraque tripartitas, y describir su rol profesional ante panel. El escenario único (Puerto de Buenaventura · Terminal de Contenedores · cliente externo Captain Lopera del BUENAVENTURA EXPRESS) se mantiene constante a lo largo de las 12 sesiones para facilitar la inmersión narrativa. La progresión CEFR A1.2→A2.1 vive INTERNAMENTE en 4 sub-bloques de 3 sesiones c/u, uno por RAP absorbido.

### 3 entendimientos perdurables
- I can communicate operationally in English with non-native vessel masters via VHF using SMCP standard phrases.
- I can coordinate tripartite port operations (yard + bridge + customs) using grammatical structures appropriate to A2.0 (modals, conditionals, passive voice).
- I can present my professional role and justify my decisions in English before a panel using A2.1 occupational structures (I'm responsible for, my role involves, I have experience with).

## A.3 Content Core (4 categorías canon)

### Technical Topics
- UNIT 1 SHIP OVERVIEW — parts of ships, types of vessels, navigational equipment (Sub-bloque 1)
- UNIT 3 PLACES IN PORT — places, locations, weather, prepositional phrases (Sub-bloque 1)
- UNIT 2 THE CREW — professions in port and aboard, commands to crew, present simple, imperative (Sub-bloque 2)
- UNIT 5 SMCP — standard spelling NATO Phonetic, message markers, standard responses (Sub-bloque 2)
- UNIT 4 IN PORT — containerization, packing/shipping/cargo handling, maneuvering: mooring/docking/departure, quantifiers, modals can/could/should/must (Sub-bloque 3)
- Síntesis ocupacional + tag questions producción + estructuras profesionales (Sub-bloque 4)

### Communicative Functions
- Naming and identifying objects in port and aboard
- Confirming SMCP messages with standard responses (Roger · Wilco · Affirmative · Negative)
- Spelling with NATO Phonetic Alphabet
- Coordinating tripartite operations (parallel commands to 2-3 actors)
- Expressing prohibition/permission/possibility/requests with modals
- Describing professional functions (habitual + momentary)
- Justifying decisions with occupational structures
- Confirming info with tag questions

### Language Functions in Context

**Sub-bloque 1 (A1.2):**
  - `Verb to be: 'This is the bridge of the BUENAVENTURA EXPRESS'`
  - `Demonstratives + plurals: 'These are the lifejackets · Those bollards are at berth 5'`
  - `Imperative + preposition IN: 'Check the bollard at berth 5 · Open the gate next to the customs office'`

**Sub-bloque 2 (A1.3):**
  - `Imperative negative: 'Don't approach the bow during loading'`
  - `Modal must: 'Vessel must wait at anchorage'`
  - `Wh-questions + present simple: 'How often does the pilot board? · When does cargo arrive?'`
  - `SMCP + NATO: 'Vessel inbound · Standby for berthing instructions · Spell call sign Charlie-Oscar-Lima'`

**Sub-bloque 3 (A2.0):**
  - `Modals should/could/might: 'You should approach starboard · We could request more lines'`
  - `Past simple: 'The vessel arrived at 06:00'`
  - `Present perfect: 'The pilot has boarded · The cargo has been inspected'`
  - `Conditional 1: 'If the wind drops, we will berth on time'`
  - `Passive voice: 'The cargo is inspected by customs'`
  - `Quantifiers: 'Move 50 containers · Stack no more than 4 high'`

**Sub-bloque 4 (A2.1):**
  - `Superlatives: 'The most critical operation is berthing'`
  - `Present perfect with markers: 'I have already inspected the cargo · I have just received the report'`
  - `Tag questions production: 'You're the supervisor, aren't you? · The pilot boarded at 06:00, didn't he?'`
  - `Occupational structures: 'I'm responsible for coordination of yard operations · My role involves supervising container handling · I have experience with VHF communication · I am skilled at berthing during low-tide conditions'`

## A.4 Integrative Task (Misión Final = entrega completa del curso)

### The CML Port Turnaround — Andrea Coordinates a Full Vessel Operation
_El Cambio de Operación del CML — Andrea Coordina una Operación Completa de Buque_

Andrea recibe orden del Capitán Restrepo de coordinar la operación completa del CML CARRIER (vessel ficticio que llega desde Shanghái al Puerto de Buenaventura) en un turno de 12 horas. La operación incluye: (1) recibir solicitud inicial vía VHF de Captain Lopera (vocabulario portuario + reconocimiento SMCP, RAP-01+02), (2) coordinar maniobra de atraque dando instrucciones tripartitas a Diego Mejía (crane operator) en yard + Bosun Romero (yard supervisor) + Captain Lopera vía VHF (gramática puerto-buque, RAP-03), (3) supervisar carga y descarga con quantifiers + modales (RAP-03), (4) presentar pitch profesional final ante panel simulado (Capitán Restrepo + agente naviero externo) describiendo su rol completo, justificando 2 decisiones tomadas durante el turno, respondiendo 2 preguntas con tag questions (RAP-04). 100% L2.

**Entregables concretos:**

## 4. S6b — ORIENTACIÓN Y RECONOCIMIENTO

### S6b-A · Devolución Cuestionario No 6 (0:00–0:30)
**TT:**
> *"Your results from the Cuestionario Consolidado. Look at your score by section. Where are you strongest? Where do you need more practice? Circle your weakest section — that's your target for the Final Mission review."*

Distribuir score sheets individuales. 10 min de revisión personal + 5 min pares (comparar).

**S6b-B · Orientación Final Mission (0:30–1:00):**
**TT:**
> *"The Final Mission. Simple: you inspect a real bay in this workshop. You write the Daily Inspection Checklist — in English. Then you report to your supervisor — me — in English. 60 to 90 seconds. You have Session 7 to prepare. Session 8 is the Mission."*

**ICQ:** *"Where do you inspect? — Real bay in this center. What do you write? — Daily Inspection Checklist. When is the oral report? — Session 8. How long? — 60 to 90 seconds. Any questions?"*

**S6b-C · Asignación de bays + equipos (1:00–1:20):**
> *"Bay assignments: Group 1 — Bay 1. Group 2 — Bay 2. Group 3 — Bay 3. Go to your group now."*

Si el centro tiene menos de 3 bays: adaptar grupos — 2 aprendices por bay en turnos.

**S6b-D · Visita preliminar al bay asignado (1:20–2:00 · 40 min):**
**TT:**
> *"Today — just look. Walk around your bay. What do you see? Use your checklist from Session 3 as a guide. Write notes in English — what conditions do you observe? Is the PPE ready? Is the toolbox organized? Is the bay door area clear?"*

💡 Instructor circula. **COACHING mode:** instructor habla solo en inglés. *"What is that tool? — A ratchet. Good. And where is it? — On the workbench."*

**S6b-E · Borrador del checklist (2:00–2:20 · 20 min):**
> *"Back in the classroom. Start your checklist draft — minimum 8 items. Use what you observed in the bay. Use the Toolbelt vocabulary. Use imperative verbs. This is your working draft."*

**WRAP-UP S6b (2:20–2:30):**
> *"For home — Session 7 preparation: complete your checklist draft and write an outline of your oral report — 5 bullets of what you'll say. Practice your report out loud. 60 seconds minimum."*

## 5. S7 — PREPARACIÓN Y COACHING

### S7-A · Revisión de borradores (0:00–0:20)
**TT:**
> *"Show me your checklist draft. Not finished — just the draft. How many items? Compare with your partner. Did you include observations — 'There is...'? Did you use imperative verbs? You have 10 minutes to improve your draft — then we go to the bay."*

### S7-B · Inspección real del bay asignado (0:20–1:20 · 60 min)
**TT:**
> *"Today is preparation day. Write your checklist. Inspect your bay in English. I will visit each group. If you have a question — ask me in English: 'What is the word for ___?' or 'How do I say ___?'"*

💡 Instructor visita cada bay group (~15 min cada uno). Preguntas de activación:
- *"What is the condition of the floor jack?"*
- *"Is there any PPE missing?"*
- *"Describe this area for me — in English."*

**Aprendices producen durante la inspección:**
- Checklist items con estado (✓ / ✗ / N/A)
- Observaciones con There is/are
- Notas para el oral report

**S7: BREAK (1:20–1:40 · 20 min)**

### S7-C · Escritura del Daily Inspection Checklist final (1:40–2:40 · 60 min)
> *"Now — clean version. Take the blank checklist. Write the final version — 8 minimum items, 2 observations minimum, Work Order header fields completed. 45 minutes."*

**ICQ:** *"Minimum items? — 8. Observations? — 2 in English. Format? — Clean, organized, readable. Time? — 45 minutes. Go."*

Instructor circula. Aplicar códigos G/V/Sp/WO en lápiz (sin interrumpir). Los aprendices pueden auto-corregir durante la escritura.

### S7-D · Preparación del oral report (2:40–3:40 · 60 min)
**TT:**
> *"Your checklist is done. Now — your report. 60 to 90 seconds. Use all 5 functions: F1, F2, F3, F4, F5. Write your script — bullets only, not word for word. Here's the model structure:"*

**Estructura sugerida Workshop Readiness Report (Final Mission):**
1. F4: *"Bay ___ is ready, Carlos / [supervisor]."* (apertura)
2. F3: *"There is/are ___ [condición]. The ___ is ___ [preposición]."* (condiciones observadas)
3. F1: *"I checked the ___. I inspected the ___."* (acciones realizadas — usando Gr3 Present Simple)
4. F3+F4: *"[Si hay un problema:] There is a problem with the ___. I reported it."*
5. F5: *"Bay ___ is ready to start. Awaiting your approval."* (cierre)

### S7-E · Coaching pairs — ensayo (3:40–4:40 · 60 min)
**TT:**
> *"Practice with your partner. One person speaks — 90 seconds. Partner evaluates with Escala No 5. Then switch. Two rounds each. I'll visit each pair."*

💡 **COACHING mode** — aprendiz B da feedback con frases del Language Bank:
- *"I think the ___ was missing."*
- *"Your F3 was very good."*
- *"Can you add one more condition?"*

### S7-F · Revisión final del checklist (4:40–5:20 · 40 min)
> *"Final 30 minutes: review your checklist with the 10 criteria from Lista de verificación No 2. Self-evaluate — do you meet all 10? Fix any issues."*

Recordar los 10 criterios (proyectar Lista de verificación No 2 de S3).

**WRAP-UP S7 (5:20–6:00 · 40 min):**
> *"Tomorrow is the Final Mission. Practice your report tonight — out loud, with your checklist in hand. 3 times minimum. Sleep well. You're ready."*

Trabajo autónomo S7: borrador outline (5 bullets) + práctica oral (3 rondas cronometradas) + revisión Gap Card de S1.

## 6. S8 — THE FINAL MISSION

### S8-A · Calentamiento + última revisión (0:00–0:30)
**TT:**
> *"Today is the Final Mission. 60 to 90 seconds. Use your checklist. Report to me. I will ask one question. That's it. First — 15 minutes to review your report with your partner. Last round of coaching. Go."*

**S8: Buffer pre-misión (0:30–0:45 · 15 min):**
Preparación mental. Aprendices revisan notas y respiración. Instructor prepara el orden de turnos.

### S8-B · Final Mission oral — rondas (0:45–2:45 · 120 min)

**⚠️ Protocolo Final Mission:**
- 60–90 segundos por aprendiz
- FLUENCY mode absoluto — no interrumpir
- 1 pregunta de seguimiento de "Carlos Mendoza" (instructor)
- Todos los demás aprendices escuchan en silencio y completan su Personal Communication Card
- Sin puntaje formal — la Final Mission es TRANSFERENCIA, no evidencia adicional

**TT:**
> *"Ready. I am Carlos Mendoza. You are Santiago Ríos. Bay 2 — inspection complete. Report to me. Go."*

Aprendiz reporta 60–90 seg. Instructor hace 1 pregunta de seguimiento:
- *"Is the lift ready?"*
- *"What about the PPE?"*
- *"How many items did you inspect?"*

✅ **Formative Checkpoint S8:** Después de cada 5 aprendices, instructor hace una pausa breve:
> *"Good progress — let's notice what's working. Three strong elements from the last reports: (1) clear bay number, (2) good use of There is/are, (3) F5 confirmation at the end. Keep going."*

### S8-C · KWL Cierre — Columna L (3:00–3:30 · 30 min)
> *"Remember Session 1? The KWL table. K = what I knew. W = what I wanted to learn. Today — L = what I LEARNED. Take 10 minutes to complete the L column alone. Then share with the group."*

Distribuir (o proyectar) el KWL grupal de S1. Aprendices completan la columna L individualmente.

Plenaria: 4–5 voluntarios leen su L más importante.
> *"What is the most important thing you learned in English in this course?"*

💡 Instructor anota las respuestas de la columna L en el KWL grupal del tablero — cerrando el ciclo de S1.

### S8-D · Gap Cards S1 vs S8 — Reflexión comparativa (3:30–4:00 · 30 min)
**TT:**
> *"Now — take your Gap Card from Session 1. (Redistribuir las Gap Cards guardadas desde S1.) Look at your numbers. Now rate yourself again — the same 6 statements. Compare. Where did you start? Where are you now?"*

Los 6 I can statements:
1. I can name 15 of the 20 key tools and areas of a diesel workshop in English.
2. I can classify types of maintenance (preventive, corrective, predictive) in English.
3. I can identify and report a safety hazard to my supervisor in English.
4. I can fill in a daily inspection checklist in English.
5. I can give basic instructions about PPE in English.
6. I can describe the current state of a service bay in English.

> *"Write: 'In January I couldn't ___. Now I can ___.' One sentence for each statement."*

Compartir: 3 voluntarios leen su reflexión en inglés.

### S8-E · Learning Contract Review + Self-Assessment (4:00–4:30 · 30 min)
> *"Your Personal Communication Card — F1 to F5. These are your phrases for the workshop. Do they work? Did you use them today? Which one was hardest? Which one will you keep using?"*

Pares: compartir 1 frase favorita por función comunicativa.

> *"Self-assessment: on the back of your Gap Card — write your CEFR level at the end of this course. A1.1 is where we started. Are you at A1.1 solidly? Are you moving to A1.2?"*

### S8-F · Cierre de guía + próximos pasos (4:30–5:00 · 30 min)
**TT:**
> *"Guía 1 — The Workshop Specialist — is complete. Look at what you did: you read a technical text in English. You wrote a daily inspection checklist. You listened to a safety briefing. You reported to your supervisor. You used 5 communication functions — in English — in a real bay. That's A1.1 — achieved."*

Señalar el KWL con columna L completa.
> *"Next guide — A1.2. The Workshop Specialist continues. More vocabulary. More grammar. More speaking. Same workshop — deeper English. You're ready."*

**WRAP-UP S8 (5:00–5:30 · 30 min):**

**Closing ceremony (breve — 10 min):**
> *"Share-out: one word that describes your English in this course. Just one word — in English."*

Cada aprendiz dice una palabra. Instructor las escribe en el board: "Progress. Ready. Confident. Workshop. Understood."

**Trabajo autónomo S8:**
> *"Last homework. Optional — but recommended: take your Gap Card comparison. Post it somewhere visible. Review it in 3 months. Write the date. Check: are you still using these phrases?"*

## 7. ANSWER KEY CONSOLIDADO

### Final Mission — No hay respuestas únicas
La Final Mission es evaluación de transferencia — no hay respuesta única correcta. El instructor evalúa:
- ¿El aprendiz comunica estado del bay comprensiblemente?
- ¿Usa vocabulario técnico reconocible del Toolbelt?
- ¿Demuestra las 5 funciones comunicativas de alguna forma?

**Criterios de calidad mínimos (no formales):**
- Bay number mencionado ✓
- 2 condiciones descritas ✓
- 1 función de cada F1–F5 presente ✓
- Responde la pregunta de seguimiento ✓

### KWL Columna L — Respuestas esperadas (range)
| Aprendiz puede decir... | Indicador de logro A1.1 |
|------------------------|------------------------|
| *"I learned 20 workshop tools in English."* | Vocabulary ✓ |
| *"I can write a daily inspection checklist."* | Writing ✓ |
| *"I can report to my supervisor in English."* | Speaking + Language Functions ✓ |
| *"I understand safety briefings in English."* | Listening ✓ |
| *"I can read technical texts about diesel tools."* | Reading ✓ |

## 8. DIFFERENTIATION NOTES

### Fast Finishers — S7
- Escribir un segundo checklist para un bay diferente al asignado.
- Preparar el oral report en dos variantes: bay listo vs. bay con problema.
- En Coaching pairs: asumir rol de "supervisor" y hacer preguntas a su par.

### Need More Support — S7
- Reducir el oral report a 45 segundos mínimo (no 60).
- Permitir uso de notas en bullets durante el Final Mission (no la tarjeta completa).
- En Coaching pairs: trabajar en grupos de 3 — dos aprendices apoyan a uno.

### Fast Finishers — S8
- Hacer el oral report sin notas ni checklist en mano.
- Añadir un "problema inesperado" al reporte: *"There is a problem with the lift — I need to report it."*
- Escribir una reflexión en inglés de 5 oraciones sobre el aprendizaje de la guía.

### Need More Support — S8
- Hacer el Final Mission con checklist físico en mano (como apoyo, no como lectura directa).
- Gap Card S8: si el aprendiz no puede escribir la reflexión en inglés — aceptar L1 parcial en columna L.
- Recordar: el objetivo es COMUNICACIÓN, no perfección gramatical. *"If they understand you — you succeeded."*

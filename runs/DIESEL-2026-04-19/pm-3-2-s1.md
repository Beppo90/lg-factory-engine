## 4. SET-UP DETALLADO (0:00–0:20)

### 0:00–0:05 · Bienvenida y presentación del universo narrativo
**Teacher Talk (TT):**
> *"Good morning, everyone. My name is ___ and this is English for the Diesel Workshop. For the next 8 sessions, we work in a place called The Diesel Workshop — Bogotá. Three people work there. (Show character cards.) This is Carlos Mendoza — Lead Technician. This is Valentina Cruz — Safety Officer. And this is Santiago Ríos — Apprentice. You are Santiago Ríos."*

💡 Mostrar tarjetas de personajes. Señalar cada uno con nombre. No traducir todavía.

### 0:05–0:12 · Objetivo de la sesión + English Zone
**TT:**
> *"Today we answer three questions: Who are we? Where do we work? What do we already know? Today we also start the English Zone — in this classroom, we try to speak English. It's OK to make mistakes. It's not OK to not try."*

💡 Escribir en pizarra: **WHO · WHERE · WHAT** — quedan como guía visual de la sesión.

### 0:12–0:20 · Activación del conocimiento previo (brainstorm libre)
**TT:**
> *"Look at this picture (workshop layout). What can you see? Tell me any word — in English or Spanish. Don't worry. Just say it."*

💡 Anotar las palabras en inglés en el margen del board plan. Las palabras en español: "Good — in English that's ___." No corregir — mapear baseline.

## 5. WHILE DETALLADO

### WHILE-A — PM-2.1 Act 1: The Headline (0:20–0:45 · 25 min)

**0:20–0:25 · Proyectar/distribuir el titular:**
> *"Three mechanics hospitalized after workshop fire — investigators blame poor PPE compliance."*

**TT:**
> *"Read this. Take 2 minutes. Don't translate every word. Find the key words: what happened? who? why?"*

💡 Escribir en pizarra: **hospitalized / compliance / investigators / workshop** — supervivencia mínima.

**0:25–0:32 · Pares — 3 preguntas (Think-Pair):**
*"Talk to your partner: (1) What happened? (2) Why did it happen? (3) What is PPE?"*

**ICQ:** *"How many questions do you answer? — Three. Do you translate the whole headline? — No, just key words. OK — go."*

**0:32–0:45 · Share + estructura target:**
Plenaria: instructor recoge respuestas. Introduce la estructura:
> *"The technician is in the hospital. The problem is poor PPE compliance."*

✅ **Formative Checkpoint 1:** Pedir a 3 voluntarios que completen: *"The ___ is in ___."* — corregir solo verbo be (ACCURACY).

---

### WHILE-B — PM-2.1 Act 2: The Map (0:45–1:30 · 45 min)

**0:45–0:50 · Proyectar/distribuir infografía del workshop layout:**
**TT:**
> *"This is The Diesel Workshop. Look — 9 areas. Let's name them together. Repeat after me: RE-cep-tion A-rea."*

💡 Señalar cada zona. Finger drilling: WORK-shop (●○), SER-vice BAY (●○ ●). Mínimo 3 repetitions por término difícil.

**0:50–1:05 · Drill de zonas (choral + individual):**
Instructor apunta al mapa → grupo dice el nombre → voluntario usa en oración:
- *"This is the ___ ."*
- *"The ___ is next to the ___."*

**1:05–1:20 · Actividad en pares — 'Describe your spot':**
*"Point to a zone. Tell your partner: 'This is the ___. The ___ is in the ___.' Two zones each."*

**ICQ:** *"Do you write or speak? — Speak. How many zones? — Two each. Do you use notes? — Yes, the map is OK. Go."*

**1:20–1:30 · Plenaria: Personajes en el mapa:**
*"Where is Carlos Mendoza? In the service bay. Where is Valentina Cruz? Near the hazmat zone."*

Introduce: *"There is a floor jack in Bay 2."* (Gr2 preview — no formal todavía)

💡 Las estructuras **be** y **there is/are** aparecen naturalmente — no corregir Gr2 aún, solo modelar.

---

### WHILE-C — PM-2.1 Act 3: The Story + The Debate (1:30–2:30 · 60 min)

**1:30–1:40 · The Story — introducción de personajes:**
**TT:**
> *"It's Monday morning at The Diesel Workshop. Carlos Mendoza is the Lead Technician — he's experienced, he's professional, he's strict about safety. Valentina Cruz is the Safety Officer — she's the PPE expert. Santiago Ríos is the new apprentice — that's you. He's nervous, but he's ready."*

💡 Mientras el instructor narra, apuntar a las tarjetas de personajes. Aprendices pueden tomar notas de palabras.

**1:40–1:50 · Debate Setup — 3 afirmaciones:**
Proyectar afirmaciones una por una:
1. *"Preventive maintenance is more important than corrective maintenance."*
2. *"A mechanic who doesn't speak English cannot read a diesel engine manual."*
3. *"PPE rules are just bureaucracy — an experienced mechanic doesn't need them."*

**TT:**
> *"You have green and red cards. Green = I agree. Red = I disagree. I'll read the statement. Vote with your card. Then explain."*

Distribuir tarjetas verde/rojo.

**ICQ:** *"Green card means? — I agree. Red card means? — I disagree. Do you need to speak? — Yes, one sentence. What sentence starters are on the board? — 'I think... because...' and 'I disagree because...' OK — first statement."*

**1:50–2:25 · Debate (3 afirmaciones × ~12 min):**
Por cada afirmación:
- Instructor lee → votar (30 seg)
- 3–4 voluntarios explican (2 min)
- Instructor recast silencioso: anotar 1–2 errores en pizarra (anónimos)
- Mini cierre: *"The correct answer in technical English is..."*

💡 **FLUENCY mode durante el debate** — no interrumpir producción. Feedback diferido al final.

**2:25–2:30 · Post-debate feedback diferido:**
> *"Let's look at two sentences from today. (Escribe anónimamente.) What is the grammar error? Let's correct it together."*

✅ **Formative Checkpoint 2:** 2 oraciones en pizarra — grupo identifica el error y propone corrección. (ACCURACY – verbo be.)

### WHILE-D — PM-2.2 Act 1: KWL Grupal (2:50–3:30 · 40 min)

**2:50–2:55 · Distribuir tabla KWL + instrucción:**

**ICQ:** *"How many columns do you complete today? — Two. Which ones? — K and W. When do you complete L? — Session 8. Go — you have 5 minutes."*

Pegar post-its con la columna W en el margen del mapa de personajes.

### WHILE-E — PM-2.2 Act 2: Safety Images (3:30–4:05 · 35 min)

**3:30–3:35 · Distribuir/proyectar las 4 imágenes:**
**TT:**
> *"Look at these four pictures from The Diesel Workshop. Each picture has a safety problem. Your job: find the problem and describe it in English."*

Estructuras en pizarra:
- *"The technician is not wearing ___."*
- *"The tools are on the ___."*
- *"There is a ___ near ___."*
- *"The ___ is blocked by ___."*

**ICQ:** *"Do you write or speak? — Both. Do you write full sentences? — Yes, use the sentence starters. How many pictures? — Four. Do you work alone? — No, in pairs. Go."*

**3:35–3:50 · Pares — describir las 4 imágenes (15 min):**

### WHILE-F — PM-2.2 Act 3: Gap Cards + Peer Interview (4:05–4:45 · 40 min)

**4:05–4:10 · Distribuir Gap Cards:**
**TT:**
> *"These are your Gap Cards. Six statements — 'I can...'. Rate yourself from 1 to 5. 1 = I can't do this yet. 5 = I can do this well. Be honest — this is NOT a grade."*

**ICQ:** *"Is this a grade? — No. What does 5 mean? — I can do this well. Do you sign it with your name? — Yes, we need it for Session 8. Go — 3 minutes."*

**4:10–4:15 · Completar Gap Cards (individual):**

Criterios Gap Card:
1. I can name 15 of the 20 key tools and areas of a diesel workshop in English.
2. I can classify types of maintenance (preventive, corrective, predictive) in English.
3. I can identify and report a safety hazard to my supervisor in English.
4. I can fill in a daily inspection checklist in English.
5. I can give basic instructions about PPE in English.
6. I can describe the current state of a service bay in English.

💡 **GUARDAR las Gap Cards.** El instructor las recoge al final y las redistribuye en S8.

**4:15–4:35 · Peer Interview (5 preguntas):**
**TT:**
> *"Now — interview your partner. You have a script with 5 questions. Ask them in English. Write their answers. 10 minutes each direction — start."*

Script de entrevista:
1. *"What tools do you use most in the workshop?"*
2. *"What type of maintenance do you do most — preventive or corrective?"*
3. *"Do you always wear PPE? Which PPE do you wear?"*
4. *"What happens in your workshop when there is a spill?"*
5. *"What English words from the workshop do you already know?"*

💡 El instructor circula, toma notas del baseline de producción oral. NO corregir — solo observar y anotar.

**4:35–4:45 · Share-out grupal (2–3 pares):**
> *"Tell us one interesting thing about your partner's workshop in English."*

## 8. DIFFERENTIATION NOTES

### Fast Finishers (Actividades 1–3 PM-2.1)
- En The Headline: buscar 2 palabras técnicas adicionales con sus raíces en latín (hospital-, compli-).
- En The Map: escribir 3 oraciones completas adicionales con preposiciones de lugar.
- En el Debate: preparar una réplica al argumento del compañero con una oración nueva.

### Need More Support
- En The Map: permitir uso de traducción del layout en español en la primera ronda; segunda ronda solo en inglés.
- En el Debate: usar exclusivamente las frases ancla del board. No exigir extensión de respuesta — 1 frase es suficiente.
- En el Gap Card: permite calificación 1–2 sin presión. Recordar: "This is your starting point — not your ending point."
- En la entrevista: el par puede leer las preguntas en voz alta del script; no requiere memorización.

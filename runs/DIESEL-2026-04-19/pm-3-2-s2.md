# PM-3.2 — S2: Read the Workshop
**Guía 1 — The Workshop Specialist · DIESEL-2026-04-19**
**Fase SENA:** Apropiación — Input Receptivo (Vocabulary + Reading) (3.3)
**PMs:** PM-2.5 (Vocabulary, Literacy & Scenario Setup) + PM-2.3 (Reading — The Master Anchor)
**Duración:** 300 min instructivos + 20 min break = 320 min pared
**L1 máx.:** 25% · **Gramática activa:** Gr1 CONSOLIDA (be) + Gr2 INTRO (There is/are)

### WHILE-B — PM-2.5 Act 2: Flashcard Battle + Category Race (0:50–1:20 · 30 min)

**0:50–1:05 · Flashcard Battle (pares, 15 min):**
**TT:**
> *"Flashcard battle. You have 20 cards. Aprendiz A shows the IMAGE. Aprendiz B says the TERM and uses it in a sentence. Then switch. You have 5 minutes each direction — go."*

**ICQ:** *"Who shows the image? — Aprendiz A first. What does Aprendiz B do? — Says the term and a sentence. How many minutes? — 5 each. Do you need to be perfect? — No, try. Go."*

💡 El instructor cronometra (5 min + cambio + 5 min). Circula y toma nota de términos con pronunciación difícil → feedback post-actividad.

**1:05–1:20 · Category Race (mesas, 15 min):**
**TT:**
> *"Now — category race. You have a table with 5 columns. Put all 20 words in the right column. You have 4 minutes. Ready — go."*

Categorías: Environment / Tools & Equipment / Safety / Maintenance / Documents & Roles

**ICQ:** *"How many categories? — Five. How many words total? — 20. Work alone or team? — Team at your table. Time? — 4 minutes. Start."*

Después de 4 minutos: verificación grupal proyectada.

✅ **Formative Checkpoint 2:** Plenaria rápida — instructor lee categoría, pide a un grupo que liste sus términos. Corrección inmediata con recast: *"Close — 'work order' goes in Documents, not Environment. Everyone: work order — Documents."*

### WHILE-C — PM-2.5 Act 3: Daily Checklist Preview (1:20–1:45 · 25 min)

### WHILE-D — PM-2.3 Act 1: Predicción por imágenes (2:05–2:25 · 20 min)

**TT:**
> *"After the break — now we read. But first: don't open the text. Look at the pictures. (Proyectar imágenes Shadow Foam.) What tools can you see? What do they do? Predict: what is the text about?"*

**2:05–2:12 · Individual → pares (7 min):**
Aprendices predicen en pares:
- *"I think the text is about ___."*
- *"I can see a ___ in the picture."*
- *"A ___ is used to ___."*

**2:12–2:25 · Plenaria de predicciones (13 min):**
3–4 pares comparten. Instructor anota predicciones en pizarra sin confirmar ni negar.
> *"Good predictions. Now let's find out if you're right. Open the text."*

### WHILE-E — PM-2.3 Act 2: Jigsaw (2:25–3:55 · 90 min)

**2:25–2:30 · Asignación de secciones y grupos expertos:**
**TT:**
> *"The text has 4 sections. Group A reads Section A — The Workshop. Group B reads Section B — Hand Tools. Group C reads Section C — Safety Tools. Group D reads Section D — The Specialist. You are experts in your section. Read — understand — prepare to teach."*

**ICQ:** *"How many sections? — Four. Do all groups read all sections? — No, only your section. What do you do after reading? — Teach the others. How much time to read? — 12 minutes. Go."*

**Secciones Jigsaw:**
- **Section A — The Workshop:** workshop, service bay, workbench, toolbox
- **Section B — Hand Tools:** wrench, ratchet, socket, torque wrench
- **Section C — Safety Tools:** floor jack, safety goggles, gloves, PPE
- **Section D — The Specialist:** specialist, preventive maintenance, work order, checklist

**2:30–2:42 · Lectura experta (12 min — individual dentro de grupo):**
💡 Instructor circula. Aprendices subrayan los términos Toolbelt de su sección. Pueden anotar 1 pregunta de comprensión.

**2:42–2:57 · Discusión en grupo experto (15 min):**
> *"In your expert group — discuss: What are the 4 key words? What does each word mean? What is one example sentence? Prepare to teach."*

**2:57–3:40 · Teach-back (grupos mixtos — 43 min):**
Reorganizar: 1 experto de cada sección por grupo nuevo. Cada experto enseña su sección (8–10 min).
- *"My section is about ___. The key words are ___. One example: ___."*

💡 El instructor circula. Toma nota de errores de pronunciación para feedback post-jigsaw. No interrumpir durante el teach-back.

**3:40–3:55 · Cuadro resumen Gr2 + PM-2.9 F3 consolidation (15 min):**
**TT:**
> *"Now — look at the text. Find all the sentences with 'There is' or 'There are'. How many did you find?"*

Construir cuadro en pizarra con ejemplos del texto:

| There IS | There ARE |
|----------|-----------|
| There is a torque wrench... | There are tools on the workbench... |
| There is a spill near... | There are three wrenches... |

> *"'There is' — one thing. 'There are' — more than one. Negative: 'There is no __.' Question: 'Is there a __?'"*

💡 **PM-2.9 F3 injection explícita aquí:**
> *"These phrases are used every day in the workshop. When Santiago Ríos reports to Carlos: 'There is a spill near bay 2.' — that's a communication function. We call it F3 — Describing conditions. You will use it in Session 5."*

- **There is + singular:** *There is a spill near the bay door.*

## 8. DIFFERENTIATION NOTES

### Fast Finishers
- **Flashcard Battle:** crear 3 oraciones nuevas usando términos que NO están en el texto de S2.
- **Jigsaw:** en el teach-back, añadir una oración con There is/are para describir una imagen de la sección.
- **Cuestionario No 1:** escribir 1 oración adicional que explique la respuesta (extensión voluntaria — no evaluada).

### Need More Support
- **Word Wall:** permitir consultar las tarjetas durante el Category Race (primera ronda). Segunda ronda: solo memoria.
- **Flashcard Battle:** reducir a 10 términos (los 5 de Environment + 5 de Safety) para aprendices con mayor dificultad.
- **Jigsaw:** para grupos pequeños (<8 aprendices): usar solo 2 secciones (A+B) y duplicar expertos.
- **Cuestionario No 1:** leer las preguntas en voz alta antes de empezar (sin dar las respuestas).

## IDENTITY AND PURPOSE

PM-3.6 is the official SENA Guía de Aprendizaje (GFPI-F-135). It is a **student-facing didactic resource** — the apprentice's learning compass. It is NOT a management document, NOT an instructor planning tool, and NOT a validation report. It speaks directly to the apprentice and delivers their step-by-step learning route through the program.

### Role in the pipeline

| Document | Role | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| PM-3.4 Student Workbook | Deepens practice with additional activities similar to PM-3.6 | Apprentice |
| PM-3.6 Learning Guide | Primary instructional route — what to do, on what, under what condition, what to deliver | Apprentice |
| PM-3.5 Final Mission | Internal instructor administration document — protocols, scoring, administration | Instructor only |
| PM-3.1 Playbook Outline | Master instructor reference — includes PM-3.5 as Appendix C | Instructor only |
| PM-3.2 Build-Out Completo | Complete session build-out — PM-3.5 embedded in S7/S8 | Instructor only |

**PM-3.5 in PM-3.6:** Only the student-facing elements are embedded in PM-3.6 as the Transferencia capstone activity: scenario text, roles table (character / title / minimum function instances), 8 task requirements, and duration (3–4 min). Do NOT include: instructor administration protocols, pre/during/post procedures, one-prompt rule, follow-up question bank, or scoring notes.

## AUDIENCE AND VOICE

**Audience:** The SENA apprentice enrolled in the program.

**Voice:** Third person formal instructive. Every instruction is executable — the apprentice can follow it autonomously without the instructor present.

**Language:** BILINGUAL — Inglés / Español throughout Section 3 (Actividades de Aprendizaje).
- All V+O+C dimension instructions appear in **both languages**: English first, Spanish immediately below.
- All numbered step-by-step instructions appear in **both languages**: English first, Spanish below each step.
- Sections 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7 remain in Spanish only (institutional/administrative content).

**Prohibited:** Descriptive narrative of what "will happen in class."
- ❌ "En esta sesión se trabajará con el vocabulario técnico..."
- ❌ "El instructor presentará los personajes del taller..."
- ✅ EN: "Identify the safety problem described in the headline and relate it to possible causes in a diesel workshop."
- ✅ ES: "Identificar el problema de seguridad descrito en el titular y relacionarlo con las causas posibles en un taller diesel."

## THE V+O+C FORMULA

Every activity instruction follows this structure:

**Verb (infinitive) + Object + Condition / Verbo (infinitivo) + Objeto + Condición**

- **Verb / Verbo:** The exact action the apprentice performs.
- **Object / Objeto:** What the action is performed on — topic, material, task.
- **Condition / Condición:** The parameter, norm, model, or context that governs how it is done.

Each V+O+C instruction is written **twice**: English line first, Spanish line second.

| Dimension | V+O+C English | V+O+C Español |
|---|---|---|
| COGNITIVA | Classify the 20 Toolbelt terms according to the five workshop categories. | Clasificar los 20 términos del Toolbelt según las cinco categorías del taller de motores diesel. |
| PROCEDIMENTAL | Build the Word Wall by physically organizing the term cards into their category, following the classification model shown in class. | Construir el Word Wall organizando físicamente las tarjetas en su categoría, siguiendo el modelo presentado en clase. |
| ACTITUDINAL | Reflect on the importance of technical English vocabulary as a tool for safe, precise communication in diesel maintenance. | Valorar la importancia del vocabulario técnico en inglés como herramienta de comunicación segura y precisa en el entorno de mantenimiento de motores. |

## THE THREE LEARNING DIMENSIONS

For each pipeline activity, **identify which dimension(s) it naturally activates** — one, two, or all three. Label only what is genuinely present. **Never fabricate a dimension that the activity does not naturally activate.**

An activity may carry:
- Only **one** dimension label → one V+O+C instruction line
- **Two** dimension labels → two V+O+C instruction lines
- **All three** dimension labels → three V+O+C instruction lines

### [COGNITIVA — Saber]
The activity mobilizes appropriation of theories, facts, concepts, or logical principles.
Thinking skills verbs: identificar, clasificar, recordar, comprender, analizar, interpretar, describir, relacionar.

### [PROCEDIMENTAL — Hacer]
The apprentice executes a practical task, follows a step-by-step process, or applies theory in the real world.
Action/creation verbs: construir, operar, reparar, producir, organizar, completar, realizar, presentar, aplicar, redactar.

### [ACTITUDINAL — Ser]
The activity focuses on behavior, ethics, responsibility, and social interaction in relation to technical work.
Reflection verbs: valorar, argumentar, reflexionar, analizar consecuencias, demostrar, comprometerse, reconocer.

## MANDATORY ACTIVITY STRUCTURE

Every activity in Section 3 must follow this exact structure. **No exceptions.** The content for each field comes directly from the corresponding PM-3.2 session JSON — the job is to TURN the instructor's plan into the apprentice's instructions, not to invent new content.

```
### [Activity Number] — [Activity Name] (EN) / [Activity Name] (ES)
Session: S[N] · Materials / Materiales: [list from PM-3.2 materials_checklist, student-relevant items only]

**[COGNITIVA — Saber]** ← only if the activity naturally activates this dimension
EN: [V+O+C instruction in English — infinitive verb + object + condition]
ES: [V+O+C instruction in Spanish — verbo infinitivo + objeto + condición]

**[PROCEDIMENTAL — Hacer]** ← only if the activity naturally activates this dimension
EN: [V+O+C instruction in English]
ES: [V+O+C instruction in Spanish]

**[ACTITUDINAL — Ser]** ← only if the activity naturally activates this dimension
EN: [V+O+C instruction in English]
ES: [V+O+C instruction in Spanish]

Step-by-step / Paso a paso:
1. EN: [Step 1 in English — student-facing, executable, drawn from PM-3.2 instrucciones_paso_a_paso]
   ES: [Step 1 in Spanish]
2. EN: [Step 2 in English]
   ES: [Step 2 in Spanish]
[... continue for all steps, omitting instructor-only actions (circulate, observe, collect) ...]

**Producto a entregar:** [What the apprentice produces and submits]
**Formato:** [Physical or digital format: worksheet, printed sheet, post-it, oral, etc.]
**Criterio mínimo:** [Minimum acceptable standard: number of items, length, accuracy threshold]
```

**Stripping rule for steps:** Remove from PM-3.2 step-by-step any action that belongs exclusively to the instructor: "instructor reads aloud," "instructor circulates," "instructor collects," "instructor writes on board." Keep everything the apprentice does.

**Deliverable rule:** Every activity — including purely preparatory ones — must have a Producto + Formato + Criterio mínimo. For preparatory activities with no formal evidence, the product is still the output of the activity (post-it, completed section of worksheet, oral answer, notes taken, etc.).

## EVIDENCE MAPPING LOGIC

Not every activity produces a formal evidence. Follow the pipeline's existing evidence design — do not add or move evidences.

### Evidence types
- **Conocimiento:** Written test or quiz assessing conceptual understanding.
- **Desempeño:** Observable performance assessed by the instructor in real time.
- **Producto:** A tangible deliverable (document, checklist, report) submitted and evaluated.

### Evidence-activity relationship
- One activity may produce one evidence.
- A group of activities may together produce one or more evidences.
- Some activities are preparatory — no evidence attached.

### When an evidence is present, specify
> **Evidencia [N] — [Type]:** [Instrument name] · [Format] · [Minimum criteria or extension]

### Section 3 — Actividades de Aprendizaje

**3.1 — Actividad de Reflexión Inicial (Análisis)**
Sessions S1–S2. Activities that activate prior knowledge and introduce the workshop context, characters, and vocabulary. Map dimension labels from pipeline activities. Close with E1 (Reading — Conocimiento) where the pipeline places it.

**3.2 — Actividad de Contextualización (Comprensión)**
Session S3. Activities that develop technical writing in a workshop context. Map dimension labels. Close with E2 (Writing — Producto) where the pipeline places it.

**3.3 — Actividad de Apropiación (Apropiación)**
Sessions S4–S6. Activities that develop listening, speaking, language functions, and culminate in the Cuestionario Consolidado. Map dimension labels. Close with E3 (Listening — Conocimiento), E4 (Speaking — Desempeño), E5 (Language Functions — Desempeño), E6 (Cuestionario — Conocimiento) where the pipeline places them.

**3.4 — Actividad de Transferencia (Transferencia)**
Sessions S7–S8. Includes Final Mission student-facing content from PM-3.5:
- Scenario text (Bay 2, Monday 7:00 AM, Training Coordinator arrives)
- Roles table: character / title / minimum function instances
- 8 task requirements
- Duration: 3–4 minutes per team

Do NOT include: administration protocols, pre/during/post procedures, one-prompt rule, follow-up question bank, scoring formula. Those live in PM-3.5 → PM-3.1 Appendix C and PM-3.2 S7/S8.

Map dimension labels for all rehearsal and performance activities. Close with Final Mission evidence (Desempeño — Escala de Estimación No 6).

### Section 4 — Evaluación
Summary table: Evidence | Instrument | Type | Session | Points.
Total: 35 points.
Score bands for Cuestionario Consolidado (E6 — Conocimiento).
One short paragraph on evaluation philosophy in student-facing language.

### Section 5 — Glosario
20 Toolbelt terms. A1.1-level definitions. Category column. Written for the apprentice.

### Section 6 — Bibliografía
Numbered APA references.

### Section 7 — Control del Documento
Version table: version / date / author / description of changes.
